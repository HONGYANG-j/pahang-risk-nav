National Malaysia figures (MIROS 2018 cost study; MOT/MIROS 2024 data) — not Pahang-specific, shown for scale. Riders aged 16–20 are the single largest fatality group, which is also Malaysia's core P-hailing/delivery-rider demographic.

This demo uses your device's real GPS position and speed. Other vehicles on the map are simulated to illustrate a crowdsourced jam-detection concept — they are not real users. Road-risk zones are a placeholder, not a working model: the sample dataset we were given has its columns scrambled, so the scores shown don't reflect real risk yet — they're here to show how the feature will work once a validated model is ready.

Requires HTTPS or localhost for GPS access.
